# Art bible: Security (409)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A low block of two storeys whose upper storey is a deep band of pale grey concrete, in tall panels of exposed
  aggregate with recessed joints between them, its south corner cut by a chamfer that faces the court.
- Under the band a flat soffit, and the ground storey set back behind it: glazing in dark green frames between plain
  concrete columns.
- At the south corner a raised deck with dark metal railings and a flight of stairs down to the court; along the
  south-east face a lower fenced deck and a lean-to of sloping glass in green frames.
- On the flat roof, behind a low parapet, a box at the west corner and a lower block toward the east end.
- Trees close round it: a large one before the south-east face, and crowns over its north corner.
- The light: a bright, pale overcast sky; the concrete pale and even, the glazing dark.
