# Art bible: Architecture - West (421W)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured from
it.

- A concrete slab in the same language as 421E beside it: pale grey concrete faces stepping in storey by storey as
  they rise; on each floor a band of windows in brown frames, and under many of them a pale louvred band.
- The top floor glazed all along under a deep hipped eave with a brown fascia and a dark soffit.
- A tower in brown vertical cladding rising out of the roof, its top falling to the north-west, with steel flues
  standing on it.
- North-west of the eave's corner a lower flat roof with a brown railing, a brown-clad corner with a rainwater head
  and downpipe, and a brown-clad block beyond.
- South-east of the slab's set-back, toward 421E, a face in dark brown cladding with dormer windows in it.
- At the bottom, glazed doors and a dark glazed ground storey under a louvred band.
- The light: a clear day, the sun from between the north-east and the east (the tower's north-east face lit, its
  north-west face in shade), the sky deep blue.
