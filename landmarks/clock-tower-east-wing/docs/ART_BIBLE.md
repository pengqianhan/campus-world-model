# The Clock Tower East Wing — art bible

Target: the Clock Tower East Wing, drawn in code so that it reads as the same building as in the
photograph `refs/streetview/EX9b…_wide.jpg` (and its `upper` and `door` views), with its proportions
from the LiDAR. Look at the photographs yourself, at full size; this page says only what they and the
2023 aerial show. What none of them shows is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.

## From the lawn and the car park (south-west)
- **Rock-faced cream stone**, the Clock Tower's own: blocks in level courses, their faces rough and
  pale cream to warm white in the sun, the joints fine. The corners of the pavilions and the tower
  are the same stone, not dressed quoins.
- **Two storeys.** Between the two pavilions the range stands back; in its middle the stair tower
  comes forward a little and rises well above the roofs, a plain tall block with no window toward the
  lawn and a flat top.
- **The pavilions** each end in a low gable toward the lawn, coped with stone; under it, upstairs, a
  pair of windows with shallow segmental heads, and downstairs a row of narrow square-headed ones.
- **The range between** has, each side of the tower, segmental-headed windows upstairs (a pair, and on the
  west side a third on the pavilion's side wall) and a row of three or four narrow square-headed ones
  below.
- **Windows**: white-painted frames and glazing bars in a light pattern (the arched heads radiate, the
  lower lights have small panes), set back in the stone; the glass dark, with the sky in it.
- **The roof**: red clay tiles on the main range, hipped at the ends, seen above the walls between the
  pavilions; the pavilions' own low roofs behind their gables are grey (the aerial).
- **The ground**: an asphalt car park right up to the walls in front, the lawn and steps beyond it.

## Light
- The photographs are of a sunny day in May 2025 (autumn): the walls a little warmer than white (mean
  colour of the building in `EX9b…_wide.jpg` about 148, 143, 136), the roof a dull brick red.
