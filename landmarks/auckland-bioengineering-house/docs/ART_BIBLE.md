# Art bible: Auckland Bioengineering House (439)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured from
it but the second fin's place, which is estimated.

- A tower clad all round in a curtain wall of bronze-tinted glass in dark frames, a grid of panes, its plan a
  rectangle with its corners cut.
- On its north face two stone fins in a pinkish brown, standing out of the glass and rising a little above its top.
- A notch cut into the tower's north-west corner over several floors, its soffits dark and the glass set back in it.
- At its foot on Symonds Street a sloping glazed roof over the entrance, a dark fascia with the building's name over
  the glazed doors, and steps up from the street.
- The light: a cloudy day with the sun breaking through, the sky white and pale blue, the glass taking the sky's
  light; the street wet.
