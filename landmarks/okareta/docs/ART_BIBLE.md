# Art bible: Okareta (215)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it.

- A villa of two storeys in the Italianate manner of the 1880s, its walls rendered and painted a warm yellow ochre,
  under hipped roofs of grey corrugated iron.
- On the left of its front a verandah across the ground floor, its roof of corrugated iron on slender posts with a
  lace frieze, three tall windows arched at their heads in moulded surrounds over it.
- On the right a canted bay of two storeys under its own hipped roof, two windows arched at their heads on each
  storey of its front, a deep cornice on brackets between the storeys and under the eaves.
- The window frames and the front door painted dark teal; a low iron fence and a garden before it.
- The light: sun through the street trees, dappling the front with the leaves' shadows.
