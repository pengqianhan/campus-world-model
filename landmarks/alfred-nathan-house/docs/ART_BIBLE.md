# Alfred Nathan House — art bible

Target: Alfred Nathan House, drawn in code so that it reads as the same building as in the five Street
View photographs in `refs/streetview/`, with its proportions from the LiDAR. Look at the photographs
yourself, at full size (`vZGe…_wide.jpg` and `vZGe…_door.jpg` for the house, `fF8q…_wide.jpg` and
`51j5…_upper.jpg` for the block); this page says only what they and the aerial show. What none of them
shows is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.

## The house (from Princes Street)
- **A two-storey villa, painted pale grey-white all over**: walls, cornices, columns and balustrades
  one colour, the shadows of its mouldings doing the drawing.
- **The front**: two canted bays of two storeys, one each side, each with a tall window in its front
  face and a narrower one in each angled side on both floors, the windows in moulded surrounds with
  shouldered (round-cornered) heads on the ground floor; a moulded string course between the floors;
  the bays' tops level with the balcony's entablature.
- **Between the bays**: the porch, up about eight steps from the front path, behind three openings
  (a wide round arch in the middle, a narrower one each side) on columns with leafy capitals; a dark
  red panelled front door with glazed panels. Over it the balcony: a balustrade of turned balusters
  bowing out toward the street, four columns, a curved entablature, and a second dark red door.
- **The top**: a deep cornice on brackets across the front and down the sides, and on it a
  balustrade of turned balusters with square pedestals at the corners and over the bays, urns on the
  corner pedestals.
- **The sides**: plain walls; on the right side (toward the library) one small window high up near
  the front (RHEq); on the left, windows seen through a tree (rA70).
- Before it, outside the model: a low white wall with cast-iron railings and square piers, an iron
  gate at the steps, a small garden.

## The block (from the campus path to the east)
- **The long side toward the library**: grey metal cladding with standing seams close together,
  no windows where the photographs see it, a step in its top near the far end.
- **The east end**: a deep grey frame (sides about 0.7 m wide, a thinner top) round three bays of
  glazing split by two slender grey fins; three storeys, each standing on a pale band of slats that
  bows out a little between the fins; the glazing behind a veil of thin vertical rods, dark glass
  with the floors' lights and blinds behind.
- **The lowest storey** stands back under the east end: white walls, glass doors to the left and a
  map board to the right, slender round columns at the corners, a wide paved landing and steps down
  to the path.
- The roof, from above only (the 2023 LINZ aerial): grey metal, its hips meeting at a plant room.

## The light (match the photographs)
- Princes Street (2024-07): a winter day with a pale hazy sky, no sun on the front; the house's front is in soft
  shade, pale grey, the doors dark red, the glass dark with pale reflections.
- The campus path (2025-05): bright low sun from behind the block (it flares in fF8q's and 51j5's
  wide views); the east end in shade, its grey frame dark against the sky, the pale bands light.
- Measure, don't guess: compare the luminance of a blank wall and of a window between a strip's
  reference and its render (crop the same region of both).

## Around it (not part of the model)
- Large deciduous trees along Princes Street in front of the house (bare in July), a tree over its
  left back corner; the General Library's long low front and its curved glass corner beside the
  block; a basalt wall along the path east of it.
