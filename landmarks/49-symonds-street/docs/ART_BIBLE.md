# Art bible: 49 Symonds Street (620)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured from
it.

- An office tower of about thirteen storeys, clad in pale grey-white panels with punched windows in bands, and on its
  stepped corners curtain walls of blue glass in dark frames, each step's face glazed with a white top.
- In the middle of each face a bay under a gable, the gable's face white and a tall window of glass arched at its top
  running down most of the bay.
- On the roof a mansard of grey slopes round a flat top, and plant.
- At its foot to the east a podium of two storeys clad in dark grey stone, a pointed gable over its entrance and tall
  windows beside it; lower buildings behind it.
- The light: a sky of broken cloud, the white panels in soft light, the glass taking the sky and the clouds.
