# Art bible: M&HS Building (507)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- Along the street, a block of five storeys: its upper storeys clad in tall near-black metal fins with windows between
  them, cantilevered over a glazed base two storeys high whose glass runs the length of the face; a recessed entrance
  under its north end, with a column at the corner.
- North of it three glazed bays step back from the street one behind another, each with dark sides, the canopy over
  the entrance at their foot.
- Behind them a north-west block under flat roofs at the same height, with raised parts, and a lower part on its
  south side; none of these faces is in the photograph.
- The roofs are grey, as the 2023 aerial shows them.
- The light: dusk, the face in shade, its windows and base lit from inside. The model draws no windows and no light
  inside, so the viewer lights the face from the front to give it the photograph's brightness.
