# Art bible: the General Library

What the photographs show (twelve Google panoramas, 2022-09 on Alfred Street, 2024-07 on Princes
Street's side, 2025-05 at the south-east end and behind) and the LiDAR measures. Draw only that; what
no photograph shows is drawn plain, at the LiDAR's sizes.

## The building
- **A modernist library of white concrete**: a tower slab of about eight floors standing across the
  south-west half of a two-storey podium, a blank white core rising through the slab's middle above
  its roof, and in front of it on Alfred Street a blank white stair tower, the tallest thing seen from
  the street.
- **The slab**: ribbon windows the whole length of each long face, floor after floor, between white
  spandrels about a metre high that stand a little proud; the glazing in slender dark (navy to black)
  frames, a mullion every metre and a half and a transom across the lower part of each pane. The
  lower floors on Alfred Street have taller glazing. At the south-east end the ground floor is glazed
  between dark grey piers, with the main entrance and the name board over it; above it two floors are
  a curtain wall of blue-grey glass in grey frames, then the ribbons again.
- **The podium's fin screens** (Alfred Street's front block, and the back wing on the north-east lawn):
  white concrete fins about 0.3 m thick standing a metre in front of the glazing, about 1.3 m apart,
  crowned at the roof's edge by a row of folded concrete hoods ("teeth"), each about three fins wide,
  widest at the top and about a third as wide at its foot, the fins running up into them, the sky
  showing between them. On Alfred Street they stand on a plinth of red-brown brick with doors and grilles
  in it; behind, on a plinth of grey render over a band of dark ground-floor glazing behind grey
  columns, with a white floor edge between.
- **The front block's end by the stair tower**: two storeys of glazing in dark frames over the brick,
  between white concrete edges.
- **The north-west end of the podium** (Qaci): a white fascia over a band of glazing with a white
  grille, and toward Alfred Street a blank panel of grey speckled stone; the slab rising behind, its
  end in ribbon windows like its long faces.
- **The glass canopy** on slender dark posts along the slab's foot on Alfred Street, in front of planted
  beds and a green fence (the fence and the planting are the street's, not the model's).

## The light
- The Alfred Street panoramas (2022-09) are of a bright spring day under thin cloud, the faces in soft
  light; the south-east end and the back (2025-05) of a clear autumn afternoon, the back wing's fins
  in full sun. The viewer's light for this building is the default sun with exposure 0.8 (config
  `light`), which brings the building region's luma median near the photographs' (142 against 137 drawn).

## Works and changes
- None of the twelve panoramas shows scaffolding, wraps or works on the building. The south-east end's
  grey piers are drawn as the 2025-05 panoramas show them, its two-storey curtain wall as the slab's
  ribbon windows and its dark stone cladding and louvre as glazing (simpler than the photographs); the
  Alfred Street front as the 2022-09 ones show it (no newer panorama there).
