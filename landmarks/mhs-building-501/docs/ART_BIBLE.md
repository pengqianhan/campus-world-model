# Art bible: M&HS Building (501)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A tower in board-marked concrete, light grey, its face banded at each floor, a strip of windows in
  dark frames up the middle of its east face, under a dark crown of standing-seam metal: a steep band over a foot
  that flares out from the shaft, its corners hipped up to a flat top.
- At its foot a glazed box of one storey in dark frames, a concrete beam along its top.
- Either side of the tower, set back from its face, glazed links of the same height as its shaft, each floor's
  glazing behind a balcony rail; the buildings beyond them pale, in metal cladding and fins.
- Before it a plaza of paving and lawn with benches, lamp posts and young trees.
- The light: a clear day, the sun on the tower's east face, the crown dark against the sky.
