# Thomas Building — art bible

Target: the Thomas Building, drawn in code so that it reads as the same building as in the photograph
`refs/streetview/S_C83uV6erL3A0gs_wide.jpg` (and its `upper` and `door` views), with `oWaF…`'s views
for the north-east end, and its proportions from the LiDAR. Look at the photographs yourself, at full
size; this page says only what they show. What none of them shows is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.

## From the road (south-east)
- **Board-marked, exposed-aggregate concrete**, warm grey-brown, darker in its recesses: the whole face
  is concrete and glass, nothing painted. In `S_C8…`'s upper view the bands read RGB 114/98/90 and the
  piers 115/105/97 (drawn in the kit's `concrete`: 118/107/97 and 121/109/99).
- **Four storeys of continuous window bands** between deep horizontal concrete bands that stand out
  from the glass: under each band its underside reads dark; each band's top edge (the LiDAR's ledges at
  2.38, 7.12 and 11.12 m) is a thin lighter line above a broad plain face. The broad parapet band at the
  top, to 15.13 m.
- **Square concrete piers**, 1.15 m wide, flush with the bands, from the plinth to the top band, 12.0 m
  apart (`FACADE.piers`: two through `S_C8…`'s fixed camera, at u 1.72 and 13.72; one behind the tree),
  and the one left of the east corner 11.2 m on, at u 24.9 and 1.3 m wide, in all three of `oWaF…`'s
  views. In `oWaF…`'s upper view a ledge runs along each band's lower edge, across the piers (not drawn:
  nothing measures how far it stands out).
- **The windows**: red-painted steel frames in a grid, mullions every 0.58 m; four rows: the ground
  storey's, short, behind the hedge, reading dark; the second, tallest (2.38-5.45 m), with two transoms
  and dark panes below the lower one; the third and the top, one transom each. In `S_C8…`'s upper view
  the rows are the ground storey's (behind the bus shelter), the second and the third. Blinds and lab
  fittings behind most panes make them read light grey (luma 123-138 at the median) more than they
  reflect the sky; the dark panes read 59.
- **The ground floor** behind a raised bed of ivy and a hedge; a bus shelter, a lamp post and a large
  tree in front (not drawn). Toward the north-east end, past a concrete post at u 17, the ground falls and
  the ground storey stands lower and taller: its band reaches down to about 1.1 m and its sills to -0.45,
  on a plinth over a lawn; a white glazed door and a white-framed louvre stand beside the pier, then
  windows and a concrete post, only a little behind the piers' face.
- Beyond the north-east end, `oWaF…` sees the corner of the face to the road and the end: the bands run
  round onto the end over windows set back behind them, as far as v 18.8, then a plain wall, then the
  glazed link and 110N beyond it (110N's model, not this one).

## Light
- The photographs are of a bright day in August 2024 with broken cloud: the face to the road is in
  shade, lit by the sky, its luma 81-189 (p02-p98) in `S_C8…`'s views, 108-113 at the median.
- The viewer's light is the shared default (estimated): no sun direction can be read from a face in shade.
