# Thomas Building — art bible

Target: the Thomas Building, drawn in code so that it reads as the same building as in the photograph
`refs/streetview/S_C83uV6erL3A0gs_wide.jpg` (and its `upper` and `door` views), with `oWaF…`'s views
for the north-east end, and its proportions from the LiDAR. Look at the photographs yourself, at full
size; this page says only what they show. What none of them shows is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.

## From the road (south-east)
- **Board-marked, exposed-aggregate concrete**, warm grey-brown, darker in its recesses: the whole face
  is concrete and glass, nothing painted.
- **Four storeys of continuous window bands** between deep horizontal concrete bands that stand out
  from the glass: under each band its underside reads dark; each band's top edge (the LiDAR's ledges at
  2.38, 7.12 and 11.12 m) is a thin lighter line above a broad plain face. The broad parapet band at the
  top, to 15.13 m.
- **Square concrete piers**, about 1.2-1.3 m wide, standing proud of the bands from the ground to the
  parapet, about 11.8 m apart (two of them through `S_C8…`'s fixed camera, at u 2.0-3.2 and 13.7-15.05).
- **The windows**: red-painted steel frames in a grid; the lower storeys' tall panes with dark panels
  in their lower third; the top storey's panes shorter. Blinds and lab fittings behind the glass make
  it read light grey more than it reflects the sky.
- **The ground floor** behind a raised bed of ivy and a hedge; a bus shelter, a lamp post and a large
  tree in front (not drawn).
- Beyond the north-east end, `oWaF…` sees the corner of the face to the road and the end, with the same
  bands, and the glazed link and 110N beyond it (110N's model, not this one).

## Light
- The photographs are of a bright day in August 2024 with broken cloud: the face to the road is in
  shade, lit by the sky, its luma 81-189 (p02-p98) in `S_C8…`'s views, 108-113 at the median.
- The viewer's light is the shared default (estimated): no sun direction can be read from a face in shade.
