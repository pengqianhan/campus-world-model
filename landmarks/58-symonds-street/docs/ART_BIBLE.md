# Art bible: 58 Symonds Street (435)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A pale grey office tower of eight storeys or so over a slope that falls to the south-east, square at its corners.
- Every floor has a deep slab's band, light grey, running round the tower; under each band hangs a row of thin
  horizontal louvres on brackets, darker grey, over a band of windows with light frames and mullions and pale panels
  under the sills.
- At the corners a plain column runs between the bands.
- At its foot a dark ground storey, set back, over a car park's ramp on the north-east side; railings, bins and a few
  palms before it.
- On the street side a core rises over the roof.
- The light: a bright day with a thin high haze, the sky pale blue; the faces pale, the louvres and windows dark.
