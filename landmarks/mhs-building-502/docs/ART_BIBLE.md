# Art bible: M&HS Building (502)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A main block of seven storeys in pale concrete. Its north face, to the street, is a row of tall pale concrete fins
  with window bays between them, each bay's spandrels in a textured grey aggregate, so that the face reads mid grey;
  its east end is pale flat panels with a near-black glazed strip beside them.
- Round its top a dark metal skirt, sloping out, under a low hipped metal roof; a white lattice on the roof at the
  east end, and a plant room at the west end.
- Beyond its west end a lower part of about five storeys with the same skirt, and a raised part beside the block.
- The light: a clear day, the east end in sun, the north face lit from the side.
