# Art bible: Building 903

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A long block of four storeys. Its two upper storeys are a box clad in pale grey corrugated metal with its ribs
  upright, a thin flashing along its top, and windows in pale frames set in it singly and in bands, flush.
- The box overhangs the two storeys under it on both the south face and the east end: a flat soffit, and under it the
  ground storeys set back, glazing in pale grey frames between plain grey piers, some of it two storeys high.
- On the south face near the corner a flat canopy over a glazed entrance, with steps and a rail up to it from the
  road; along the face planters and a low wall with a rail.
- The south face steps forward about 30 m from the corner, where three standpipes rise to the roof; a single one
  further along.
- The east end's top is a very low gable within a parapet.
- The light: a bright overcast sky; the cladding pale and even, the glazing dark with the rooms and their lights
  behind it.
