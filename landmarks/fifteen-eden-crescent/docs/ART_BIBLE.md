# 15 Eden Crescent — art bible

Target: 15 Eden Crescent, drawn in code so that it reads as the same building as in the photograph
`refs/streetview/MH15…_wide.jpg` (and its `upper` and `door` views), with its proportions from the LiDAR.
Look at the photographs yourself, at full size; this page says only what they show. What none of them
shows is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.

## From Eden Crescent (north-east)
- **Two storeys of white panelled walls**, long and low along the street, the panels in a square grid
  with fine joints.
- **The windows** are set deep in square openings, one a bay, in two rows; their frames and sills are
  red-orange.
- **The base**: brownish-grey tiles from the footpath to about 1 m above it, a band of white tiles over
  them, a terracotta band at the sills. Near the north-west end: a red fire door with steps under a tall
  window, a louvre over a small green hood with a red-framed glazed case under it, and a red-brown door
  of boards with a glazed panel and steps.
- **Under the canopy**, along the street face's top and set back in its shade: a band of dark windows
  with red mullions. The top storey, under a curved roof, stands back behind the sloping roof and is not
  seen from the street.
- **The north-west end** meets 9 Eden Crescent (801), a taller white building with wide glass bands.

## Light
- The photographs are of a sunny day in August 2024 (late winter), the sky blue with white cloud. In
  `MH15…`'s the street face is in the sun, the light falling on it at a slant from the north-west: the
  street tree's shadow lies on the wall and the base beside the tree, and each opening's head throws its
  shadow across the jamb below it.
- The viewer's light (the config's `light`, its note) is not read from them: it is 9 Eden Crescent's,
  inferred, a sun 305° round and 25° up that stands just behind the face's plane, so the viewer draws
  the face in shade, without the tree's and the heads' shadows (estimated); its exposure is set so that
  the white paint's brightness matches the photographs'.
