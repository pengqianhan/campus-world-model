# Art bible: 2-6 Park Ave (529)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A long block of four storeys under a thin flat roof with a dark eave standing out over its faces.
- Its east face, on Park Avenue: at each upper storey a balcony along the whole face on a pale slab, its front banded
  with thin louvres, a light rail of two bars on posts at its edge; behind the balconies bands of dark windows.
- The LiDAR shows the same balconies on the north, south and west faces, which the photograph does not show.
- The ground storey in pale grey brick, its windows under the first balcony's slab; before it a planted bed of shrubs
  and young trees, a sign post and the footpath.
- On the roof grey plant, and a raised part with a red roof.
- The light: an overcast day, soft, without shadows; the sky white.
