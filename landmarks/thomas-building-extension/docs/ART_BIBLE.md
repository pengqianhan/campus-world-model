# Thomas Building Extension — art bible

Target: the Thomas Building Extension, drawn in code so that it reads as the same building as in the
photographs `refs/streetview/oWaF…_wide.jpg` and `MOQA…_wide.jpg` (and their `upper` and `door` views),
with its proportions from the LiDAR. Look at the photographs yourself, at full size; this page says only
what they show. What none of them shows is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.

## From the road (south-east)
- **A curved glass curtain wall** round the east of the building, three storeys of it over a base: tall
  panes about 2.1 m wide between slim green-glass fins, each storey's panes under a light aluminium head
  and a dark transom band; the glass is pale and green-grey, full of the sky and the trees it reflects,
  with the offices' window frames and ceiling lights seen through it.
- **A dark steel beam** under the glass, along the whole curve, standing a little proud of the base.
- **The base**: white-painted rendered concrete, with creeper on it; a set of louvred service doors with
  dark glazed side lights near the flat face, and small square windows further round.
- **The flat south-east face** beside the curve: grey board-marked concrete, the boards running up,
  over a concrete band at the curtain wall's foot, and white render with creeper under that. A
  University banner hangs on it (not drawn).
- **Beyond it**, to the left in `oWaF…`'s view: the glazed end of the link to the Thomas Building, and
  110 itself (not this model).

## Light
- The photographs are of a bright overcast day in August 2024: white and grey cloud with some blue, soft
  shadows, the glass the brightest thing on the building after the sky it reflects.
- The viewer's light (the config's `light`, its note) is estimated: no sun direction can be read from
  soft shadows.
