# Art bible: 67 Symonds Street (619)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A white office tower of about eight storeys over the street on its south, clad in large pale panels with fine
  joints, on a podium that runs down the slope behind it.
- Its south face: a plain stretch at its west end, then a curved bay of dark glass bands, then three columns of square
  dark windows a storey, and plain panels to the south-east corner.
- Its east face: a louvred panel, then three windows a storey, behind a large tree.
- Over its roof a pale block with plant, and a dark sloping panel near the south-east corner.
- At its foot, an entrance porch with a glass pyramid over a covered walkway from the street, with signs; a car park's
  entry beside it.
- The light: an overcast day, the east face brighter than the south, the sky white.
