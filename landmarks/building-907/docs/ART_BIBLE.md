# Art bible: Building 907

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A hall under a shallow barrel vault, clad in white ribbed metal; its end toward the street shows the vault's arc as
  its top edge. At the foot of that end a band of glazing in dark frames, a pale strip across its middle; two dark
  louvred openings high up, one each side of the crown.
- Beside it, nearer the street, a two-storey annex in bright orange render, square windows in dark frames on each
  floor, a pale metal cap along its top, which falls away from the hall; a downpipe at its corner.
- Round them a yard of wet asphalt behind black gates and a rusted steel pillar; a car park building and an office
  block behind.
- The light: an overcast, wet day; no shadows, the white cladding about as bright as the sky's lower edge.
