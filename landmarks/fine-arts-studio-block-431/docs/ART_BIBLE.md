# Art bible: Fine Arts Studio Block 431

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A long block of three storeys, its south face over a lane and a car park, its north side on a garden higher up the
  slope. A roof of two pitches in light grey metal: the north one rising to a step over the middle, the south one
  falling from it to a plain beige fascia with a gutter over the lane.
- The south face: beige-grey cladding panels, columns every few metres between groups of three windows in white
  frames; a row of short upper windows under the eave, a row of tall windows with transoms, and over the ground storey
  a row of short high windows. The ground storey: grey roller doors, a dark painted plinth, and a low box with two
  roller doors and a side door standing out from the face.
- At the east end, a glass stair tower beside the entrance, under a glass canopy, with a brick wall beside them
  (the second photograph, of the east entrance); the entrance faces a small court.
- On the north side, a lower annex; no photograph shows it.
- The light: broken cloud, the south face in soft light with no hard shadow.
