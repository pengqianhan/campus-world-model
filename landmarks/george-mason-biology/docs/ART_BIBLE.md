# Art bible: the George Mason Biology Building (106)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A long block of three storeys in cream render. Its south-east end, on the street, is square to it: three bays
  between plain pilasters, red-framed windows on the two upper floors (the upper ones with arched heads), a plain
  ground floor under them, and a parapet over the top.
- Behind the parapet, set back, a grey attic in vertical metal cladding with a dormer over the end, and a low grey
  hipped roof over it.
- The middle of the long block is wider, with two wings to the south-west and raised parts over its roof; low parts
  stand round its foot. None of these faces is in the photograph but the end's.
- Plane trees stand in front of the end and over the street; ivy covers its south-west corner.
- The light: a bright day, the sun behind the building and the end in soft shade, the sky hazy white low down.
