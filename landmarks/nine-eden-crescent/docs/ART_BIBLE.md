# 9 Eden Crescent — art bible

Target: 9 Eden Crescent, drawn in code so that it reads as the same building as in the photographs
`refs/streetview/v8ia…_wide.jpg`, `mdk1…_wide.jpg` and `MH15…_wide.jpg` (and their `upper` and
`door` views), with its proportions from the LiDAR. Look at the photographs yourself, at full size;
this page says only what they show. What none of them shows is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.

## The street end (north-east, on Eden Crescent)
- **White cladding**, smooth panels with fine joints, painted white; in the photographs' open shade
  it is nearly as white as the sky behind it.
- **The top storey** is a white face across the whole end, its top raked down at the south-east
  corner, with one long band of windows in it; it comes down over the storeys below to about 9.6 m.
- **Three bays below it**, set back about 0.3 m between two white piers 1.0-1.1 m wide: in the left
  and centre bays a band of windows on each of the two storeys; in the right bay a band upstairs and a
  taller window below it with a transom.
- **Downstairs**: on the left a vehicle way, open and dark inside; in the middle the glazed entrance
  from pier to pier, with a single door and a pair; on the right a glazed corner under a glazed
  canopy that runs past the end's north-west edge.
- **Windows**: dark aluminium frames and mullions, no sills; the glass light, reflecting the sunlit
  buildings across the street.

## The rest
- The long sides stand against their neighbours, and the far end faces a slope: plain white walls.
- Along the south-east side the top storey stands back behind a terrace with a low parapet.
- On the flat roof, grey plant: a plant room and two low units (the LiDAR; no photograph shows them).

## Light
- The photographs are of a sunny day in August 2024 (late winter): the street end, facing
  north-east, is in open shade, the sun behind the building (in the afternoon, inferred from that, not
  read from the photographs); sunlit patches on the street in front.
  The camera exposed for the shade, so the cladding is near white (building region luma median
  237-253) and the glass and shadows are mid-grey. The viewer's `light` in the config draws it so.
