# Music School — art bible

Target: the Music School, drawn in code so that it reads as the same building as in the photograph
`refs/streetview/qru8CkT4PN7yNixn_wide.jpg` (and its `upper` and `door` views), with its proportions from
the LiDAR. Look at the photographs yourself, at full size; this page says only what they show. What none
of them shows is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.

## From the car park (north-east)
- **A long wall of pale cream roughcast render**, painted, in full sun: no window on it. Its top steps up
  toward the north corner, in steps of a few hundred millimetres, from just over the canopy at its
  south-east end to about 8.3 m at the north corner (the LiDAR: 5.5, 6.7 and 8.3 m, with short flights of
  steps between).
- **A curved canopy of white corrugated metal** along the whole face, a quarter-round in section, its
  corrugations running down the curve; dark specks on it (dirt). Under it the same render down to the car
  park, a few small fittings (a round lamp, red marks), and a green handrail along the foot.
- **The auditorium beyond it**, to the left: a taller wall painted ochre-yellow, with a tall window
  near its left end and a light gutter along its top, under a grey roof.
- Over the parapet, a mass of ivy and shrubs on the courtyard side (not drawn), and far behind it Te
  Pūtahi Tangata under construction with a crane (a neighbour, not drawn).

## From above (the 2023 aerial, the LiDAR)
- The auditorium's roof: grey metal, in facets with seams running out from its high point.
- The north wing's flat roof: grey, in segments round the courtyard.
- The south range, the south-east part, the north-west block and the west corner: pale roofs; a glazed
  roof at the west corner.

## Light
- The photograph is of a clear day in September 2022: the face to the car park is in full sun (its
  luma's median 246, the render reads near white), the sky deep blue.
- The viewer's light is the shared default (estimated).
