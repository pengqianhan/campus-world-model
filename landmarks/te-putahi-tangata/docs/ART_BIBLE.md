# Art bible: Te Pūtahi Tangata (201)

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- On the street, a hall whose front is glass from the pavement to the roof, in tall panes between slender mullions,
  under a folded roof: plates rising and falling across the front, their ridges running back from the street, dark
  underneath and at their edges, standing out over the glass. A flat pale canopy over the entrance, left of the
  middle. Three plane trees stand in front of it and hide most of the roof.
- At the hall's north-east end, a taller block clad in pale metal in narrow vertical panels, its corner by the hall
  rounded.
- Behind them, down the slope, a long block whose roofs stand about 25 m over the street, with raised strips and
  parts, a stair's core on its south-east side and a low podium beside it; none of these faces is in the photograph.
- The roofs are grey, as the 2023 aerial shows them; the hall's roof has strips of glazing and a planted part.
- The light: a bright day, the front in soft light under the trees. The viewer's sun stands behind the camera, low
  in the north-west, to give the photograph's brightness.
