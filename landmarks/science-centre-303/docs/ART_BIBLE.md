# Art bible: building 303 of the Science Centre

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference, not a source of sizes.

- A long range along the street, about four storeys, faced in bands of brown-grey precast concrete panels (a
  warm, slightly purple brown, the panels' joints faint), each band a storey, with a shadowed gap under each band.
  Along its lower band, a large relief of white blocks in a maze-like pattern on a copper-coloured ground: an
  artwork, not to be drawn after (plain panels there). Under the lowest band, a canopy along the entrance, a grey
  rounded fascia, over the ground storey's glazing in dark frames; a forecourt with steps and a stone wall at the
  north-east end.
- Behind the range, set back, a taller slab of about ten storeys; behind it lower blocks. Only the range's face
  to the street is in the photograph; the rest is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.
- Grey flat roofs (the 2023 aerial: pale ribbed metal over the range, brown sawtooth roofs over the north-east
  block).
- The light: an overcast sky in the photograph, no shadows; the faces even in tone.
