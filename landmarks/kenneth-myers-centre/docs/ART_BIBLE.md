# The Kenneth Myers Centre — art bible

Target: the Kenneth Myers Centre, drawn in code so that it reads as the same building as in the
photograph `refs/streetview/0Rjk296LTsGDt_gX_wide.jpg` (and its `upper` and `door` views), with its
proportions from the LiDAR. Look at the photographs yourself, at full size; this page says only what
they and the 2023 aerial show. What none of them shows is drawn plainly at the LiDAR's sizes.

## From Shortland Street (south-east)
- **A Romanesque brick building** of one tall storey to the street: warm red-brown brick in level
  courses, darker and redder than the Fisher Building's tan brick.
- **Pilasters** of brick stand out from the faces at every bay and at the corners, and run up past the
  parapet into **pinnacles**: square brick shafts that end in short pyramids with small cream caps.
- **The cornice**: under the parapet a frieze of **small round arches** on corbels (an arcaded corbel
  table), then a **cream coping** with a small crenellated or toothed edge. The cream is the render's:
  warm, pale, not white.
- **Tall blind arches** fill each bay of the faces: round-headed, recessed into the brick, their
  springers marked by small cream blocks; above them a second row of smaller arches under the cornice.
- **The porch**, between the west part and the east part, is the entrance: a deep cream **arched
  door** (a round arch of several cream orders on cream colonnettes, the number "74" in its tympanum)
  over **steps** up from the street, and above it three tall arched windows with cream colonnettes;
  two big brick pilasters with pinnacles flank it.
- **The west part**, left of the porch, is the same brick with pilasters and pinnacles and tall blind
  arches; the steps and their cream side walls run along its foot.
- **The east part**, right of the porch and set back from it, has six or seven bays of blind arches
  between pinnacled pilasters, a dark brown downpipe or two, and a cream plinth along its foot.
- **The mast** behind the building, in the middle of the roof: a red and white lattice tower.
- **The roofs** are flat (the LiDAR) and not seen from the street.

## Light
- The photographs are of a bright, cloudy day in April 2026 (autumn), the street face in soft light;
  glass towers behind and round the building fill most of the sky.

## Never
- No photograph, crop or trace in the model or a commit; no people, cars, fences, signs, banners or
  the construction site's things.
- No pinnacle, arch or window that the photographs do not show; no brick on what is render.
