# Art bible: building 302 of the Science Centre

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. Sizes are in `parts/common.js` and
`docs/CONTRACT.md`; this is how it should read.

## From the street corner (the photograph)
- A tall block of glass and metal over a lower block of dark screens. The tower's south-west face is in
  horizontal bands: pale, slightly green glass in long ribbons between bands of pale grey metal, a band of
  vertical slats at the top. Its south-east face is a deep screen of brown vertical fins from the top down.
- Before the tower's south-west face, the lower block (about seven storeys) is wrapped in dark, almost black
  folded screens, like chevrons, whose folds catch the sky. A tall triangle of glass rises in its south-west
  face to near its top, the screens folding round it. The screens run on round its ends along the tower's two
  end faces, behind the trees on the south-east side.
- At the street the ground storey is glazed, under a long thin canopy on the left.

## From above (the 2023 aerial, the LiDAR)
- Grey roofs, square with the tower's faces: its main roof falling gently to a band of three sawtooth ramps across
  its middle, plant in four boxes, a raised strip along its north-east side; lower roofs at its two ends, each
  rising in a ramp to the south-west parapet, and over a small annex; the lower block's roof well below the
  tower's.

## Light
- The photograph is under a bright overcast sky: no hard shadows, the glass pale with the sky it reflects, the
  screens dark.
