# Art bible: Fine Arts Studio Block 433

What the building looks like, for whoever draws a part of it. The photograph is a reference: no size is measured
from it but the ones its contract names, which are estimated.

- A long block of four storeys under a shallow vault of light grey metal with a white fascia and gutters, its ends
  bowed out; its north-west face over the lane it shares with 431, its south-east side on lower ground among trees
  above the motorway.
- The north-west face: a dark red ground storey with blue doors and white-framed windows; over it, up to the eave, a
  wall of glazing, most of its panes pale and frosted, some red, yellow or blue, between blue-grey mullions and floor
  bands; a strip of yellow weatherboards at each end.
- The west-south-west end: yellow weatherboards over the red ground storey, with small square windows and top-hung
  windows; in the middle a pale grey bay with a red door at each upper floor onto a balcony with a bowed front and a
  blue-grey mesh railing; to the south-east an outside stair of the same steel, zigzagging down beside the balconies, on
  slim posts. The vault's curved edge stands out against the sky.
- At the east-north-east end a lower block rounded in plan and a plant room on the vault; no photograph shows them.
- The light: an overcast sky, the faces in soft light with no shadow.
