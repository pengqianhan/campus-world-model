# Campus Explorer film

## Style Prompt

A Chinese campus exploration trailer. Open on the Clock Tower's carved crown, reveal the
eight-school scope, then move from walking to a scooter to flight. Return to the Clock Tower
for the main reveal. Use real local application captures and the repository's landmark
renderer. Keep the building visible while each short statement arrives. The viewer should
want to open the map and recognise a place. Use the existing project's dark and amber
palette, as defined in `src/ui/styles.css`. The default is a cinematic opening followed by
confident, rhythmic motion. Deliver both portrait and landscape versions, each 46 seconds.

## Colors

- `#0f1b25`: canvas and solid caption plates; the application's ink.
- `#eef3f6`: primary text; the application's text.
- `#ffb454`: emphasis, large figures and navigation marks; the application's accent.
- `#a3b3bf`: secondary text; the application's muted text.
- `#14222e`: inset panels; the application's solid panel.

The captured campus keeps its own colours. No university brand colours or logos are added.

## Typography

- Microsoft YaHei: Chinese statements, 900 weight; local Windows font, not redistributed.
- Consolas: English mode names and factual labels, 400 weight.

English labels are a small instrument voice, while Chinese statements invite exploration.
Headlines are at least 66 px. Body text is at least 26 px. Credits are at least 22 px.

## Motion

Use short crossfades between related shots, with a horizontal push into the school overview
and a longer dissolve into the Clock Tower reveal. Every scene has a separate entrance
for its footage, statement, label and detail. Use expo, power and sine eases. No exits precede
a transition. Camera paths are finite and repeatable. Music has a pulse at 120 BPM, with a
short breath before the architectural reveal. The final address stays readable for five seconds.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not invent campus data, classes, rooms, usage counts or user testimonials.
- Do not show the unmerged book game as a released feature.
- Do not imply that all eight schools have floor plans or detailed landmarks.
- Do not use stock campus footage, university logos or generated building imagery.
- Do not obscure the Clock Tower's crown with text, or use flashing glitch effects.
